About WanderLux

A small Melbourne agency started by travel guides who wanted planning to feel personal again.

Our story

WanderLux opened in 2026 on Spencer Street with four consultants and one idea: every trip should be planned by someone who has actually been there.

Online booking sites are fast, but they can't tell you which Kyoto ryokan is quiet at night or which Santorini suite catches the sunset. We combine first-hand knowledge with transparent pricing, so you can see an estimate online and then refine it with a person.

We plan domestic and international holidays for couples, families, groups and solo travellers, and we stay on call while you travel.

Our mission

To plan holidays that fit the people taking them: honest about cost, careful with details, and there for you until you're home.

Customer service values

  • Listen first

    We ask how you like to travel before we suggest where. Your pace, budget and interests shape the plan.

  • Clear prices

    Estimates are published online and quotes are itemised. No surprise fees after you book.

  • Always reachable

    Your consultant's direct number works while you're away, with after-hours cover for emergencies.

  • Travel responsibly

    We favour locally owned hotels and guides, and offer carbon offsets on every flight we book.
